# ERIS Research

Research exists to improve technical decisions.

## Research process

Question
→ sources
→ compare approaches
→ identify evidence
→ identify uncertainty
→ choose experiment/implementation
→ evaluate

## Evidence levels

1. Published claim
2. Reproduced/independently verified evidence
3. ERIS experimental evidence

Never present level 1 as level 3.

## Research records

Important research should record:

- question
- sources
- date
- approaches considered
- relevant findings
- uncertainty
- decision
- experiment/result

Research should not become an excuse to avoid implementation.
